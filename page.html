About Us

Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple mission: to bring our neighborhood together over warm, handmade baked goods. What started as a small home kitchen operation has blossomed into a community-centered staple. We pride ourselves on being a welcoming space for commuters, families, and friends to gather and connect.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Great baking starts with great ingredients. We are committed to supporting our local agricultural community by sourcing our organic flour, fresh dairy, and seasonal fruits directly from regional farmers. We believe that using sustainable, locally sourced ingredients ensures the highest quality while giving back to the community we love.

Meet Our Team

Our dedicated team of bakers and staff work tirelessly to make every visit special. From our head baker, who arrives long before dawn to tend to our sourdough starters, to our friendly front-of-house staff who always greet you with a smile, we are a family passionate about sharing our craft with you.
